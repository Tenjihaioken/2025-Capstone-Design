The textures were made from pictures I have taken with my smartphone in a park near my (old) place in Stuttgart - Germany. I masked the leafs with gimp and edited the leafs texture inside paint.net and blender. The normal, ao and height maps were made with materialize. The models were made with blender (version 3.2).

- Textures: 1k and 512x512 (1k = default)
